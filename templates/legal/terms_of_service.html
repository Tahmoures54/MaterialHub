1. Introduction

At MaterialHub, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, share, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website, services, and applications (collectively, the "Service"). By using MaterialHub, you consent to the practices described here. For more on your use of the Service, see our Terms of Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver and enhance the Service. The types of information include:

  • Personal Information: During registration, we collect your company name, company email, company phone number, full name, country, company address, and role (e.g., Project Manager or Supplier).
  • Authentication Data: We store a TOTP secret for two-factor authentication, securely linked to your account via Microsoft Authenticator.
  • Usage Data: We track how you interact with the Service, including pages visited, materials listed or requested, and team management actions.
  • Technical Data: We collect device details, browser information, IP addresses, and cookies to improve functionality and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service, including account setup, authentication, and material management.
  • Enable transactions and communication in the Material Marketplace.
  • Enhance the Service by analyzing usage trends and user feedback.
  • Send essential updates, such as policy changes or service alerts (non-optional communications).
  • Protect security through TOTP authentication and monitoring for suspicious activity.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it in these cases:

  • With Other Users: In the Material Marketplace, your company name and contact details may be shared with other users (e.g., suppliers or buyers) to facilitate transactions.
  • Service Providers: We engage trusted third parties (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics) who process data under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, such as to comply with a court order or protect our rights.

5. Security of Your Information

We implement robust measures to protect your data, including:

  • TOTP Authentication: Microsoft Authenticator generates unique codes every 30 seconds, preventing unauthorized access.
  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols.
  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel access your data, with regular system audits.

While we strive for maximum security, no system is infallible. If you suspect a breach, contact us immediately via the in-app support feature.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information via account settings.
  • Deletion: Request account deletion, subject to legal obligations.
  • Objection: Object to certain data uses (e.g., analytics), where applicable.

Contact us via the in-app support feature to exercise these rights. We’ll respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to improve your experience, such as saving preferences and analyzing usage. You can manage cookies via browser settings, but disabling them may limit functionality.

8. Third-Party Links

Our Service may link to third-party sites (e.g., Microsoft Authenticator’s download page). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information.

9. Children's Data

MaterialHub is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that a child’s data has been collected, we will delete it promptly. Contact us if you believe we have such data.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, including the United States, where our servers are located. We ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws, using safeguards like standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Significant updates will be posted on the Service or communicated via email. Continued use of the Service after changes indicates your acceptance.

12. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, use the in-app support feature or visit our Contact Support page. Additional resources are available in our Help Center.

13. FAQs About Privacy

What data does MaterialHub collect?

We collect personal details (e.g., name, email), authentication data (TOTP), usage data, and technical data to provide and improve the Service. See Section 2 for details.

Does MaterialHub sell my data?

No, we do not sell or rent your data. We only share it with other users for transactions, with service providers under strict agreements, or as required by law.

How can I delete my account?

Contact us via the in-app support feature to request account deletion. We’ll process it within 30 days, subject to legal obligations.

Are cookies required to use MaterialHub?

Cookies enhance functionality but are not strictly required. Disabling them via browser settings may affect your experience.

Is my data secure with MaterialHub?

Yes, we use TOTP authentication, encryption, and access controls to protect your data. See Section 5 for more details.
