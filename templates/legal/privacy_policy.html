1. Overview

MaterialHub is a construction material operations platform. It helps teams manage engineering requests, procurement, purchase orders, deliveries, warehouse receipts, quality information, traceability and material reconciliation.

A practical principle

We aim to collect information that is needed to provide, secure and improve MaterialHub. We do not sell personal information.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, company, country, address and role when provided during registration or account administration.
  • Workspace information: project, material, supplier, requisition, purchase order, delivery, warehouse, quality and traceability records entered by authorized workspace users.
  • Authentication information: credentials and, where enabled, two-factor authentication information such as a TOTP secret required to protect an account.
  • Operational and audit information: actions and timestamps needed to operate workflows, maintain accountability and troubleshoot the service.
  • Technical information: browser/device information, IP address, request identifiers, security events and similar information generated when the service is used.
  • Support information: information you choose to provide when contacting support or requesting assistance.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and maintain MaterialHub and its workflows.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts and workspaces.
  • Keep project, procurement, delivery and warehouse records connected and traceable.
  • Provide reports, reconciliation views, sharing features and operational notifications.
  • Monitor reliability, performance, security and abuse.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service, security or policy changes.
  • Improve the product using appropriate operational and aggregate information.

4. Sharing and disclosure

MaterialHub does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be disclosed when necessary to provide the service or in the following circumstances:

  • Within your organization: information may be visible to authorized users according to workspace permissions and roles.
  • When you intentionally share a report: MaterialHub provides read-only report sharing for supported records such as MR, PO, Delivery and Warehouse reports. Anyone who receives a valid share link may be able to view the information contained in that report until the link expires or is revoked.
  • Service providers: hosting, infrastructure, security, monitoring or other providers may process information on our behalf as needed to operate the service.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect rights, security and the service.
Shared reports are different from public pages.

Shared reports are designed as focused, read-only documents. They do not intentionally include the normal site navigation, footer promotions or advertising elements.

5. Workspace and tenant data

MaterialHub is designed around organization/workspace boundaries. Authorized users should only access records permitted by their account and role. Workspace data may include business information belonging to your organization, suppliers and project participants.

Organizations are responsible for assigning appropriate users, roles and permissions and for ensuring that information entered into the service is appropriate for their operational and legal requirements.

6. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. These include authentication controls, role and access controls, CSRF protection, security headers, protected sessions and monitoring/logging capabilities.

Where two-factor authentication is enabled, TOTP provides an additional authentication factor. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

MaterialHub may use cookies or similar browser storage to maintain sessions, remember necessary preferences and support security and application functionality. Some pages may also use browser-side storage for local interface preferences.

You can manage cookies through your browser. Restricting required cookies may prevent parts of MaterialHub from working correctly.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and operational records, meet contractual or legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

When an account or data is deleted, some information may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security, backup or audit purposes for an appropriate period.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing of personal information, or request a copy of information associated with your account.

To make a privacy request, contact MaterialHub through Contact Support. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

10. Third-party services and links

MaterialHub may rely on third-party infrastructure or services to host, secure, monitor or deliver parts of the platform. Third-party websites and applications linked from MaterialHub have their own terms and privacy practices. Review those policies before providing information to them.

11. Children's data

MaterialHub is a business-oriented service and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact support.

12. International use

MaterialHub may be accessed by organizations and users in different countries. Depending on hosting and service-provider arrangements, information may be processed in a country other than the country where you or your organization is located. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for applicable cross-border processing.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, our practices or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published. For material changes, we may also provide notice through the service or other appropriate communication channels.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns about how information is handled, please use Contact Support. You can also visit the Help Center.

15. Privacy FAQ

Does MaterialHub sell personal information?

No. This policy states that MaterialHub does not sell or rent personal information.

Who can see my project data?

Access is intended to be limited to authorized users according to the workspace, account and role permissions configured for the service.

What happens when I share a report?

A supported report can be shared as a read-only link. Treat the link as sensitive because anyone who obtains a valid link may be able to view that report until it expires or is revoked.

Can I request deletion or correction?

Yes. Contact Support with the relevant account or workspace details. We may verify the request before processing it.

Is security guaranteed?

No online service can guarantee absolute security. MaterialHub uses multiple security controls, but users should also use strong credentials, enable available authentication protections and report suspicious activity.
