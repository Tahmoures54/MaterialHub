Help - Using Warehouse Operations

Welcome to the Warehouse Operations Page!

This page helps you manage warehouse inventory, material receipt, and issuance to contractors. Here's a quick guide:

  • Receive Material: Click "Receive Material" to add new stock from delivery orders. Add multiple rows using "Add Row". Fill in the details and submit (Warehouse only).
  • Issue to Contractor: Click "Issue to Contractor" to allocate materials. Ensure sufficient stock before submission (Warehouse only).
  • Edit Inventory: Use the Edit button on inventory cards to modify stock details (Warehouse only).
  • Delete Inventory: Use the Delete button to remove stock entries (Warehouse only).
  • Approve Operation: Project Managers can approve or reject warehouse operations using the Approve button.
  • Import from CSV: Use the "Import from CSV" option in the Receive Material modal to upload a CSV file (Warehouse only).
  • Export to CSV: Click "Export to CSV" to download the inventory as a CSV file.
  • OPI Dashboard: Monitor inventory accuracy, stock turnover, and low stock alerts.

If you encounter issues, check the browser console (F12 > Console) for errors or contact your system administrator.
