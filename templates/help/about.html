THE MATERIALHUB STORY

When the whole project is awake, material control should be awake too.

MaterialHub was created from a simple construction reality: a project can have skilled people, powerful equipment and detailed engineering — and still lose time because nobody has one clear answer to a simple question: Where is the material?

Online and connected Any device Security by design One connected material trail

A familiar day on a busy project site

The contractor is waiting. The crew is ready. A large crane is standing on the work front. Engineering has already issued the requirement. But the material is not there.

Someone says it was ordered. Someone else is checking an Excel file. Procurement is searching messages for the latest supplier quotation. The supplier asks which revision is correct. The warehouse has a delivery note, but nobody can immediately connect it to the original requirement.

There is no single dramatic failure. Instead, there are dozens of small information gaps — and together they become delay, rework, shortage, unnecessary calls and uncertainty.

One question. What was needed, what was ordered, what was delivered, what was actually received — and what is still missing?
THE OLD WAY

Information was everywhere. Control was nowhere.

Scattered files

Requirements, quotations, POs and delivery records live in different folders, spreadsheets and document versions.

Too many conversations

Status is reconstructed from calls, messages, emails and repeated follow-ups instead of one shared record.

Dependence on people

The person who knows the latest status may be in a meeting, on another site, unavailable or on leave.

MaterialHub is built around a different idea: the project should not have to ask people where the truth is. The operational record should already be there.

THE MATERIALHUB WAY

One connected story from Engineering to Warehouse

Engineering Need→MR→RFQ / Tender→PO→Delivery→Warehouse→Balance

Know what is missing

Compare required, ordered and received quantities. Shortage, partial receipt, not ordered and surplus become visible.

Find the answer quickly

Follow a material from its engineering requirement through purchasing, delivery and warehouse receipt without rebuilding the story manually.

Keep evidence with the work

Reports, documents, identifiers and operational records stay connected so the team can review the same facts.

ALWAYS AVAILABLE

Online. From anywhere. On the device you already have.

The office, the site, the warehouse

MaterialHub is a web-based workspace. The goal is simple: the material record should not be tied to one office computer, one folder or one person's memory.

With an internet connection, authorized team members can access the workspace from a desktop, laptop, tablet or phone — whether they are in the project office, at the work front or checking a delivery.

Everyone awake, even when someone is not

Projects do not stop because one person is away. Material status should remain visible when a purchaser is in a meeting, a warehouse colleague is off shift, or a project manager is visiting another work front.

MaterialHub turns personal knowledge into shared operational information — so work can continue without waiting for a single person to answer.

SECURITY & CONTROL

Access should be controlled. Data should stay in context.

Role-based access

Different project roles need different responsibilities and visibility. Access is designed around authenticated users and role-aware workflows.

Tenant-aware data

Company and tenant context is carried through operational records so one organization's material data is not casually mixed with another's.

Controlled sharing

When a report needs to be shared externally, MaterialHub can create a clean read-only report link for supported records rather than exposing the full application.

DESIGNED FOR REAL WORK

Less searching. Less asking. More control.

Engineering

Raise and follow material requirements with quantities, dates, priorities and project context.

Procurement

Keep RFQ, supplier and PO activity connected to the requirement that started the purchase.

Delivery & Warehouse

Record what arrived and what was actually received, then make the balance visible to the whole team.

MaterialHub does not replace the people who run a project.

It gives those people a common place to see what is happening. It does not take lunch, go on leave, disappear into another meeting or keep the latest status inside one person's phone. The purpose is simpler: keep the material story available, connected and understandable — wherever the project team is working.
