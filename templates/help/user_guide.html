How to use MaterialHub

Use this guide as the operating manual for Engineering, Procurement, Suppliers, Quality, Delivery, Warehouse and Project Management.

Control Center

Start each working day here. Review open requisitions, PO pipeline, deliveries, low stock, high-risk materials and actions requiring attention.

Material Requisition

Use one requisition per coherent requirement. Enter a precise item code, specification, quantity, unit and required date. Avoid free-text duplicates because analytics depend on consistent coding.

RFQ & Quote Comparison

Generate RFQs from demand, invite qualified suppliers, capture commercial offers and compare them using price, lead time, availability and supplier score. The cheapest quote is not automatically the best quote.

Purchase Orders

Keep promised delivery dates accurate. Late or incomplete POs feed the risk engine. Update changes rather than creating duplicate records.

QR / Barcode Traceability

Assign a trace code to each controlled material record. Capture heat, lot, serial and batch identifiers whenever applicable. Use the trace history to answer where a material came from and where it was issued.

MTC / CoC / Datasheet

Attach document references to the material trace record and keep document status explicit: Missing, Received, Under Review, Accepted or Rejected. Do not mark a material ready for issue while mandatory quality evidence is missing.

Warehouse

Receive against expected deliveries, verify quantity and condition, assign a location, scan/record trace identity and update stock. Mobile/PWA workflows are designed for warehouse execution where connectivity may be intermittent.

Material Intelligence

The intelligence layer combines stock, open orders, required dates, supplier behavior and lead-time information. Use the Material Readiness Index as an operational signal, not as a substitute for project judgment.

AI Material Copilot

Copilot should produce evidence-based recommendations. A useful answer identifies the material, current stock, open PO, required date, risk drivers and a specific action. Example: 12-inch CS Pipe may become a schedule risk in 9 days because the open PO is 11 days late and available stock is below the required quantity. Recommended action: expedite the PO or activate a qualified alternate supplier.

Governance rule: AI recommendations are decision support. Procurement, Quality and Project Management remain accountable for approvals and commercial/technical decisions.
