Getting Started

A practical onboarding path for a new project team.

1. Set up2. Register demand3. Procure4. Receive5. Trace & store6. Monitor risk

1. Set up the project

Confirm the project, user roles, supplier records, warehouse locations and material coding convention. Keep item codes stable because they become the backbone of traceability and analytics.

2. Register material demand

Create Material Requisitions with item code, description, quantity, required date and project/work package. The required date is critical for schedule-aware risk analysis.

3. Run procurement

Generate an RFQ, invite qualified suppliers, capture quotes and compare price, availability, lead time and supplier performance. Convert the selected offer into a Purchase Order.

4. Receive and control

Track expected delivery, actual receipt, inspection status and supporting documents. Use the 3-way match to compare PO, receipt and invoice quantities/values before payment approval.

5. Trace and store

Create QR/barcode trace records and capture heat, lot, serial or batch numbers. Attach MTC, CoC and datasheet references. Record warehouse location and material status.

6. Monitor risk

Open Material Intelligence and review Material Readiness, supplier OTIF, lead-time signals and schedule-aware procurement risks. When a material becomes critical, follow the recommended action and document the decision.

Recommended operating rhythm: Engineering updates demand daily; Procurement reviews open RFQs/POs daily; Warehouse updates receipts and stock continuously; Project Management reviews the risk radar at least once per day.
