MaterialHub Help & Support

Get answers to your questions and learn how to make the most of MaterialHub!

Getting Started

  • Sign Up: Create an account by visiting the Registration Page.
  • Log In: Access the Project Dashboard with your email and TOTP code.
  • Dashboard Overview: Explore your personalized dashboard to manage materials, requisitions, and more.

Using MaterialHub

  • Material Requisitions: Submit and track material requests via the Material Requisition page.
  • Purchase Orders: Create and manage orders in the Purchase Order section.
  • Warehouse Management: Monitor inventory and stock levels in the Warehouse section.
  • Quality Control: Ensure material quality through the Quality Control page.
  • Material Marketplace: Buy or sell materials in the Material Marketplace.
