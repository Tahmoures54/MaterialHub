Help - Using Purchase Orders

Welcome to the Purchase Orders Page!

This page helps you manage purchase orders for your projects. Here's a quick guide:

  • Create a New PO: Click "Create New PO" to open a form. Add multiple rows using "Add Row". Fill in the details and submit (Purchase only).
  • Manage a PO: Use the Edit/Delete buttons on each card to modify or remove orders (Purchase only).
  • Approve a PO: Project Managers can approve or reject POs in each stage using the Approve button.
  • View Details: Click on a PO No link to open a detailed report in a new tab.
  • Search POs: Use the search bar to find purchase orders by PO No or Description.
  • Import from CSV: Use the "Import from CSV" option in the Create modal to upload a CSV file (Purchase only).
  • Export to CSV: Click "Export to CSV" to download the orders as a CSV file.

If you encounter issues, check the browser console (F12 > Console) for errors or contact your system administrator.
