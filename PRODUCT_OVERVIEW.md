# MaterialHub Product Overview

MaterialHub is positioned as a **Material Operations Platform for Construction & EPC**.

### What makes the platform different
- End-to-end material lifecycle rather than a standalone inventory register.
- Procurement intelligence combining price, lead time and supplier performance.
- Traceability for heat, lot, serial and batch controlled materials.
- Quality evidence awareness through MTC, CoC and datasheet tracking.
- Schedule-aware procurement risk.
- AI-assisted operational recommendations with explainable evidence.
- Mobile/PWA-friendly warehouse workflows for field execution.

### Target users
Project Managers, Engineering, Procurement, Suppliers, Quality, Delivery and Warehouse teams.

### Product north star
> Every critical material should have a clear answer to **what is needed, where it is, whether it is technically acceptable, when it will arrive, and what action is required if it will not arrive in time.**
